## Product boundaries recorded

- Better Days is a wellbeing habit and reflection tool, not a diagnostic or
  treatment application.
- Profiles are limited to Starting Point, Building Habits, and Maintaining
  Progress.
- Serious-concern responses use a separate support path and cannot be hidden by
  a weighted score.
- Recommendations are small, respectful, and non-clinical.
- Community stories require moderation and reporting before publication.
- Wellbeing data requires consent, minimization, access control, export,
  correction, auditability, and deletion.
- AI recommendations are future work and cannot make diagnostic, crisis, or
  autonomous safety decisions.
