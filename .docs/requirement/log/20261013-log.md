## Guardrails applied

- The 50/50 tree split shows equal importance, never a score; there is no
  overall health score.
- All sample figures carry "Demo data" labels; assessment comparisons say
  "Demo data — not your actual results." An empty view previews a new account.
- Metric tiles say whether a value is self-rated, from the activity log, or
  calculated.
- Missing data shows "Not enough data yet" instead of a guessed result; sleep
  consistency needs at least three logged bedtimes.
- Trends are neutral text (no red/green), and copy avoids diagnosis, hydration,
  nutrition-adequacy or cognitive-improvement claims (LR5, LR8).
- No calories, weight or exercise targets are tracked.
- Reflection text is never shown on the dashboard (LR3, LR4 for the future
  Supabase design).
