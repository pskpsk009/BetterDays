# User Guide - BetterDays

How to use the BetterDays app. No account is needed for anything in this
guide except the optional backup (Section 7).

BetterDays is a wellbeing habit tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or judge
how you are doing. If you ever feel unsafe, contact someone you trust or a
local emergency or support line.

## 1. Open the app

1. Install **Expo Go** from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Scan the QR code the team shares (see
   [deployment-notes.md](deployment-notes.md)).
3. The first time, three short welcome screens explain what the app is and
   how your data is handled. You can pick what you would like help with, or
   tap **Skip**. The **i** button on Home shows them again.

## 2. Home

- **The figure:** tap the **head** for Mind, the **body** for Body, or the
  **legs** for Movement Trail.
- **"What do you need right now?":** tap a need (for example *Stressed* or
  *Tired or sluggish*) to see three small activities. Tap one to open it
  straight away.
- **Profile button** (top): privacy rules, download or delete your data, and
  the optional account.

## 3. Mind

Two sections, switched at the top:

- **Mental Wellness:** Self-Acceptance, Discover Your Joy, Purpose in Life,
  Live the Life (for example Breathing Bubble), Find Your Focus.
- **Emotional Wellness:** Stand Up Again, Self-Awareness, Self-Control,
  Social Creatures.

Open a branch, then an activity. Most activities keep nothing after you leave.
Three remember what you enter **on your phone only**: My Support Circle,
Values Compass, and Develop Your Skill. Each shows what is saved and has a
**Delete** button.

## 4. Body

Four activities: Hydration Check-in, Build a Balanced Plate, Daily Stretch,
and **Sleep Schedule Guidance**.

**Sleep Schedule Guidance:**

1. Set your current bedtime, preferred bedtime, and wake-up time.
2. The app suggests small steps to move your bedtime gradually.
3. Each morning, log when you went to bed and woke up.
4. See your last 7 nights and how many were on schedule (within 30 minutes or
   1 hour, your choice).

## 5. Movement Trail

1. Choose **Outdoor** (GPS) or **Indoor** (step sensor).
2. Tap **Start**. The app asks for location or motion permission the first
   time.
3. **Pause**, **Resume**, or **Stop**. The trail, distance, time, and steps
   are shown.
4. Optional: share the trail drawing to the public gallery. Shared trails
   do not include your name, and anyone can see them, so share only trails
   you are happy to make public.

## 6. Progress

A Mind-and-Body Growth Tree built from the activities you finished on this
phone. Tap Mind or Body for details. There is no overall score. A labelled
demo view shows what it looks like with more data.

## 7. Your data (Profile)

| Action | Account needed? | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy rules | No | Plain-language summary of how data is handled |
| Download my data | No | Saves a copy of everything as a file (JSON) through your phone's share screen |
| Delete all data | No | Removes everything saved on the phone, plus any backup and shared trails |
| Create account / Sign in | - | Email and password |
| Back up to my account | Yes | Off by default; asks for consent first; copies your saved data to your account |
| Delete account | Yes | Removes the account and all of its data |

When you sign out, the app asks whether to keep or remove the data on the
phone.

## 8. Tips

- Use made-up text when trying the app for a test.
- The map and live GPS only work on a real phone.
