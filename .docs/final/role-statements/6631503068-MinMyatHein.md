# Role Statement - Min Myat Hein (6631503068)

**Role:** Documentation and Research

In this project I was involved in the documentation and research workflow for the Better Days wellbeing companion app (a mobile app specifically for students and young people). The app is designed to support users to reflect on their daily routines, identify patterns and make clinical recommendations. My role was to manage and maintain the project charters, privacy and safety policy, feature backlog, design documents, user journeys, prototypes, test records and acceptance evidence. I ensure that project decisions are traceable from user research through to requirements, design, development and testing. I conduct user interviews and surveys to understand users’ needs, routines, challenges, expectations and experiences of wellbeing tools. I take care to collect feedback, anonymise it, identify the key themes and use the validated findings to inform the requirements and to prioritise the right features. My documentation work also ensures that the key principles are well documented including consent, privacy, data minimisation, secure access, data export and deletion, moderation and user safety. I also produce diagrams for each system architecture. The role is to link the research to the product and to keep the project well documented, well organised, evidence based, user centred and in line with its wellbeing aims.

## Reflection

Working on the BetterDays application with AI was a good learning experience for me. I realized that AI can make software development easier and faster, especially when we need ideas, design suggestions, or help with coding problems. During this project, we used AI to explore different ideas and speed up development. One thing that improved was our development speed because AI helped us save time and reduce repetitive work. However, one disadvantage was that we sometimes depended too much on AI. Not all generated code worked correctly or matched what we wanted, so we still had to understand the code, test it, and fix problems ourselves. I also learned that teamwork and communication are just as important as technical skills because building an application requires everyone to understand their responsibilities and work together.

At this point, I see myself as a student who is still learning and preparing to enter the software industry. I don't think knowing how to code is enough anymore, especially in the AI era. Software engineers also need to know how to solve problems, think critically, and use AI tools properly. I believe AI will become a normal part of software development, but we should not depend on it completely. We still need to understand the work and take responsibility for what we build.

Next year, my main goal is to complete my internship and get experience working in a real company. I want to improve my technical skills, learn from experienced developers, and understand how real software projects are managed. I also plan to work on more personal projects to improve my portfolio. In five years, I hope to have a stable career as a software engineer, with enough experience to handle projects independently. I know technology will keep changing, so I want to continue learning and improving myself along the way.
