## Reflection

Using Ai  in software engineering sectors, will always have good and bad aspects.The bad aspects i learnt from this project would be, I am confident to say that especially in planning phase,we humans will need to have strong goal in mind,like how we want our project to go,we must have clear direction before asking ai to plan our project. This is because without the solid structure, we would be just following the AI direction,so it will be more like AI is leader and we are follower, it should be the other way around.If we keep following the plan AI gave, our projects will be lacking to attract more users, because it is AI-tailored,not human. Therefore, it will be hard to communicate well with our users.For coding,selecting architecture,which technology stack to use, instead of asking AI what to use, the best choice would be, we choosing the stack that fit with our projects,because,  each stack has their own unique advantages and disadvantages. For coding part, even if we do not write own codes and instead vibe code, we knowing how our project work, which code do what function, will always provide the strong advantage for every developer in future phases. Not knowing which code do what, would prove difficult to maintain our projects in future when we will be needing to fix bugs or improve , it will be very hard to do.One thing that got better in our project because of ai  will be the speed of how fast we developed this. Bad thing is,like i mentioned above,bug fixes and improving functions were difficult because we rely heavily on AI when we are in early development phases.
