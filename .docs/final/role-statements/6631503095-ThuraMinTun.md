**Role:** Project Owner and Frontend Developer

For this project, I made the product vision, scope, objectives and weekly plan for better days. ## Reflection

Lessons learned. Building BetterDays with AI changed how I worked as both project leader and frontend developer. The biggest improvement was speed. With AI help I built the Home screen, 27 Mind activities, the Body and Trail screens, the Growth Tree, and the Profile sheet much faster than I could have alone. Writing clear rules in CLAUDE.md and rule.md also improved quality. Because the AI followed our privacy and safety rules, such as no diagnosis and consent before backup, requirements and design became the most important part of my job, not typing code. One thing got worse. AI makes unfinished work look finished. When our user-testing logs were still empty, it was tempting to fill them with made-up answers just to show progress. The AI refused, and that taught me that real evidence, like interviews and test results with real users, cannot be generated. As a leader I also found that fast AI changes can leave documents like the README out of date, so someone must keep checking that everything still matches.

Where I stand today. In the AI era I see myself less as someone who only writes code, and more as someone who decides what to build, guides the AI, and checks its work. Skills that AI does not replace, such as understanding users, setting scope, protecting privacy, and leading a team, matter more now. I still need to improve backend security.

My plan. Next year, I want to improve my skills as a frontend developer, gain practical experience through an internship, and build and publish my own applications. I also want to develop a deeper understanding of JavaScript, TypeScript, React, and modern web development technologies. In five years, I hope to become a successful software engineer or senior developer who can lead development projects, create useful applications, and use AI tools to work more efficiently while maintaining quality, honesty, and user safety at the centre of every project.
